Update v5.x:
(Brewmaster)
1. A hero is created, he has I GOT DRUNK* and I GOT TIRED abilities.
2. I GOT DRUNK: makes Brew larger and reverse the movement. While he's large, he cannot run and jump. Get's ability I'M TIRED.
3. I'M TIRED: Brew drops to the ground smashing everything. The ball slips if it was underneath Brew's belly.

(Dark Seer)
1. A hero is created. Has VACUUM and ION SHELL abilities.
2. VACUUM: pulls everything in radius towards the center of the AoE. Hero pulling power is lower. The cast range is low.
3. ION SHELL: a bit of reworked RICKOSHET ability. The cast range is low.

(Sand King)
1. A hero is created. Has a LOW DASH and SAND STORM abilities.
2. SAND STORM: creates a sandstorm in a small AoE, which slows the ball and all heroes in it. Also unlocks the LOW DASH ability.
3. LOW DASH: pushes the hero to the target point while slowing and tossing everything (including the ball) above you, simultaneously reducing all momentum to zero.

(Void Spirit)
1. A hero is created. Has VOID FORM and REMNANT abilities. They have connected cooldown (that means, using one ability makes another go on cooldown either way).
2. VOID FORM: fades away and creates rings under his foot. Choosing the ring makes you immediately teleport to its center. If you hold the ball, ability to raise the ball in the air makes it travel to your chosen position.
3. REMNANT: choose the target point and vector; Remnant creates one there and kicks or pulls a hero who steps in its way. BKB for the ball for 2 seconds, no friction.
